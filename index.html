Yinqi Yang

I’m a PhD student in Philosophy at Rice University. Before coming to Rice in 2024, I earned my BA from New York University, where I majored in Philosophy and Anthropology and Classical Civilization. I think and write about various topics in philosophy, including moral psychology, (meta)ethics, agency, and epistemology.


I’m very sympathetic to the idea that philosophy should be approached interdisciplinarily, and so empirical and theoretical work from other disciplines has a special place in my heart. One of my current intellectual challenges is figuring out how to take empirical findings in psychology and neuroscience seriously without losing the philosophical spine.

Why is Notice-and-Consent Problematic? (AI Ethics)

Why does clicking “I agree” feel so different from signing a consent form at the doctor’s office? I argue it’s not because we skip the fine print, but because consent does its moral work only under trust, and our relationships with data collectors aren’t ones in which trust is warranted.
